Visit Portsmouth Spinnaker Tower

Here's a summary of the famed Spinnaker Tower!

The Spinnaker Tower is an iconic 170-metre (558 ft) landmark observation tower located on the waterfront of Portsmouth, England. Opened in 2005 as the centerpiece of the Gunwharf Quays and Portsmouth Harbour redevelopment, its distinctive design reflects the city's rich maritime heritage by mimicking a billowing spinnaker sail. The tower features three observation decks—including a glass "Sky Walk" floor located 100 metres above sea level and an open-air "Sky Garden" at 110 metres—that offer sweeping, 360-degree panoramic views spanning up to 23 miles across Portsmouth, the Solent, the Isle of Wight, and the South Downs.
