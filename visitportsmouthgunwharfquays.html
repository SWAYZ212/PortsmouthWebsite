What is Gunwharf Quays?

Gunwharf Quays is a premier waterfront shopping and lifestyle destination located in Portsmouth, England, right next to the historic harbor. Developed on the site of a former Royal Navy ordnance yard, it successfully blends its rich naval heritage with modern leisure.

  • Designer Outlet Shopping: Home to over 90 high-end fashion and lifestyle brands offering significant discounts, making it a major retail hub on the south coast.
  • Dining and Entertainment: A vibrant selection of waterside restaurants, bars, and cafes, alongside a multi-screen cinema, bowling alley, and casino.
  • The Spinnaker Tower: Soaring 170 metres into the sky above the harbor, this iconic landmark offers breathtaking panoramic views of Portsmouth, the Solent, and the Isle of Wight.
  • Maritime Atmosphere: Preserved historic cranes and fortifications from its days as a naval armaments depot, creating a unique backdrop overlooking Portsmouth Harbour and HMS Warrior.
